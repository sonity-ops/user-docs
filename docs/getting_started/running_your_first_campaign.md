# Run your first campaign

Once you have [set up two-factor authentication](/docs/getting_started/setting_up_2fa) and [added a profile](/docs/getting_started/adding_profile), you are ready to run your first **Connector Campaign**.

### Step 1: Click the New button

From the Home page, click the **New** button to create a campaign.

![Click the New button](/images/create_connector_campaign/s1-click-the-new-button.png)

### Step 2: Select a Connector Campaign

Choose **New Connector Campaign** from the menu.

![Click New Connector Campaign](/images/create_connector_campaign/s2-click-new-connector-campaign.png)

### Step 3: Add a campaign name

Give your campaign a name. You can also pick a seed campaign if you have one.

![Add campaign name](/images/create_connector_campaign/s3-add-campaign-name.png)

### Step 4: Find your contacts

Do a search on LinkedIn (or Sales Navigator), then copy the search URL.

:::tip
In my case the search was simple but you can add more filters to it so that its much more specific.
:::

![Do a search and copy the URL](/images/create_connector_campaign/s4-do-a-search-and-collect-and-copy-url.png)

## Step 5: Paste the URL and continue

Paste the search URL into the campaign and click **Next**.

You can also add contacts using **profile URLs** or a **CSV import**.

![Paste search URL and click next](/images/create_connector_campaign/s5-paste-in-search-url-and-click-next.png)

### Step 6: Add steps and continue

In the  **Steps** stage, configure your message flow, then click **Next**.

For more information on steps? See [Campaign Steps](/docs/campaigns/campaign_steps).

:::tip
You can add as many steps as you like. And you can also use AI to generate the steps as highlighted in the image (check green highlight)
:::

![Add steps and click next](/images/create_connector_campaign/s6-add-steps-and-click-next.png)

### Step 7: Create the campaign

Review your campaign and click **Create campaign**.

![Create campaign](/images/create_connector_campaign/s7-create-campaign.png)

### Step 8: You are up and running

Your campaign has been created. Monitor its progress from your campaign reports – see [Monitoring Campaigns](/docs/campaigns/monitoring_campaigns).

![Campaign created](/images/create_connector_campaign/s8-the-campaign-has-been-created.png)

For deeper concepts, explore the [Campaigns](/docs/category/campaigns) section.


### FAQ
#### What happens after I have created the campaign?
Sonity will launch a Search and Collect so that it can gather the contacts. After that is done, set it to Active and Sonity should run the campaing against the contacts
