# Add a profile

Before Sonity can act on your behalf it needs a **profile** connected to your LinkedIn account. A profile represents the account that performs actions within your campaigns.

> Make sure you have completed [Set up two-factor authentication](/docs/getting_started/setting_up_2fa) first.

To add a profile, follow the steps in [How to add a profile](/docs/managing_your_account/how_to_add_a_profile).

## Add your profile

With 2FA enabled, you can now add your profile in Sonity.

### Step 1:  Head over to profiles page
Under profiles page click the Add button
![Add your profile button](/images/linked_in_2fa/s10-adding-new-profile-part-1.png)

:::tip
On new accounts Sonity will automatically ask you to Add a New Profile
:::

### Step 2: Add profile details

A dialog should popup and you should be able to add a new profile then click Add

![Add your profile](/images/linked_in_2fa/s10-adding-new-profile.png)

## Next steps

Once your profile is added, you are ready to [run your first campaign](/docs/getting_started/running_your_first_campaign).
