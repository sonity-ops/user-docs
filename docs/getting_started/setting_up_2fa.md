# Set up Two-Factor authentication

Before you add a profile, you need to make sure two-factor authentication (2FA) is set up on your LinkedIn account. This keeps your account secure and prevents automation from pausing when LinkedIn asks for a verification code.

Follow the steps below to enable 2FA on your LinkedIn account.

### Step 1: Select your profile

From the **Me** icon in the top-right of LinkedIn, open your profile.

![Select your profile](/images/linked_in_2fa/s1-select-profile.png)

### Step 2: Open Settings

Go to **Settings & Privacy** from your profile menu.

![Select settings](/images/linked_in_2fa/s2-select-settings.png)

### Step 3: Open Two-step verification

In the **Sign in & security** section, click **Two-step verification**.

![Click two-factor authentication](/images/linked_in_2fa/s3-click-two-factor-authentication.png)

### Step 4: Set up two-step verification

Click **Turn on** (or **Set up**) to enable two-step verification for your account.

![Set up 2FA](/images/linked_in_2fa/s4-setup-2fa.png)

### Verify your account

LinkedIn will ask you to verify your identity before you can continue.

![Verify account](/images/linked_in_2fa/s4b-verify-account.png)

### Step 5: Choose an authentication method

Select an authentication method, such as an **authenticator app**.

![Select authentication method](/images/linked_in_2fa/s5-select-authentication-method.png)

### Step 6: Enter your password

Enter your LinkedIn password to continue.

![Enter password](/images/linked_in_2fa/s6-enter-password.png)

### Step 7: Scan the code and save

Scan the QR code with your authenticator app and save your backup settings.

![Scan code and save](/images/linked_in_2fa/s7-scan-code-and-save.png)

### Step 8: Enter the code and continue

Enter the six-digit code from your authenticator app and click **Continue**.

![Enter code and continue](/images/linked_in_2fa/s8-enter-code-and-continue.png)

### Step 9: Two-step verification is set up

Your two-step verification is now enabled on your LinkedIn account.

![2FA done](/images/linked_in_2fa/s9-2fa-done.png)

## Next steps

Your account is now secured. The next step is to [add a profile](/docs/getting_started/adding_profile).
