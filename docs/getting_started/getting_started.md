# Getting started

This guide takes you through setting up Sonity so you can run your first LinkedIn automation campaign.

## What you will need

- A LinkedIn account you want to automate from
- Sonity running in your browser

## Quick overview

1. **Set up two-factor authentication (2FA)** – secure your LinkedIn account before connecting it.
2. **Add a profile** – connect your LinkedIn account so Sonity can act on your behalf.
3. **Run your first campaign** – create a campaign, add contacts, and define steps.

Follow the pages in this section in order. Each page walks you through one piece of the setup:

- [Set up 2FA](/docs/getting_started/setting_up_2fa)
- [Add a profile](/docs/getting_started/adding_profile)
- [Run your first campaign](/docs/getting_started/running_your_first_campaign)

Once you are set up, see [What are campaigns](/docs/campaigns/what%20are%20campaigns) to learn more about what you can build.
