# How to read and act on analytics

Estimated reading time: 6 minutes.

Analytics tell you whether your campaign is healthy and where to improve.

## Key metrics to monitor

1. Connection acceptance rate.
2. Reply rate.
3. Step completion rate.
4. Error or fallback usage rate.

## How to act on low performance

1. Low acceptance rate:
   Improve targeting and connection message quality.
2. Low reply rate:
   Refine message relevance and CTA clarity.
3. Low completion rate:
   Reduce delays that are too long or remove friction in the sequence.
4. High fallback usage:
   Fix missing personalization fields in contacts.

## Review cadence

Check campaign performance daily during the first week, then move to a weekly optimization cycle.

For event-level analysis, review [Campaign metrics](/docs/integration/metrics).
