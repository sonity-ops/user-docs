# What are Campaigns

Campaigns are organized efforts designed to achieve a specific goal, such as promoting a product, reaching a target audience, or increasing engagement over a set period of time.

## Types of Campaigns
### 1. [Connector Campaigns](./connector_campaign)

Connector Campaigns are designed to send connection requests to targeted prospects, helping you grow your professional network and start building relationships with potential leads.

### 2. [Messenger Campaigns](./messenger_campaign)

Messenger Campaigns allow you to send direct messages to your connections, making it easier to nurture relationships, follow up with prospects, and share relevant information in a personal way.

### 3. [InMail Campaigns](./in_mail)

InMail Campaigns are used to reach people outside of your direct network through LinkedIn InMail, enabling you to contact high-value prospects even if you are not yet connected.

### 4. [Schedule Post Campaign](./schedule_post)

Schedule Post Campaigns help you plan and publish content automatically at selected times, ensuring consistent posting and improving audience engagement without requiring manual updates.

### 5. [Auto Accept Campaign](./auto_accept)

Auto Accept Campaigns automatically accept incoming connection requests, helping you expand your network faster and maintain momentum in your outreach efforts.

### 6. [Flow Campaign](./flow)

Flow Campaigns help you structure outreach in a more natural way by spacing interactions and combining actions such as connection requests, messages, and other engagement steps.
