# Campaign Lifecycle

![Create a schedule posts campaign](/images/campaign_lifecyle.png)

A campaign in Sonity follows a structured lifecycle, from planning your outreach to scaling what works.

Understanding this lifecycle helps you build more effective campaigns and continuously improve results.

## Overview

Every campaign moves through the following stages:

`Plan -> Build -> Launch -> Engagement -> Optimization -> Completion -> Repeat / Scale`

This is not a one-time process. It is a loop. Each campaign teaches you something you can apply to the next.

## 1. Plan

This is where your campaign begins.

Define:

- Your target audience (for example founders, recruiters, developers)
- Your goal (lead generation, hiring, networking)
- Your messaging angle

A strong plan sets the foundation for everything that follows.

## 2. Build

Create your campaign inside Sonity.

This includes:

- Importing contacts (search, CSV, profile URLs)
- Defining your sequence:
  - Connection request
  - Message
  - Follow-ups
- Setting delays and timing between actions

This step turns your idea into an automated workflow.

## 3. Launch

Activate your campaign.

Once launched:

- Connection requests are sent automatically
- Messages follow based on your defined sequence
- Activity runs within safe daily limits

At this stage, your campaign is live and running.

## 4. Engagement

This is where real interactions happen.

Your contacts may:

- Accept your connection request
- Reply to your messages
- Ignore the outreach

You may need to:

- Respond to replies
- Qualify leads
- Move conversations forward

This stage directly impacts your results.

## 5. Optimization

Review and improve your campaign performance.

Monitor:

- Acceptance rate
- Reply rate
- Drop-off points in your sequence

Adjust:

- Messaging content
- Target audience
- Timing between steps

Optimization is what turns an average campaign into a high-performing one.

## 6. Completion

A campaign reaches completion when:

- All contacts have been processed
- You manually stop or pause it
- Your goal has been achieved

At this point, you can evaluate results and prepare for the next iteration.

## 7. Repeat / Scale

Use what you have learned to improve and expand.

You can:

- Duplicate and refine successful campaigns
- Target new audiences
- Run multiple campaigns simultaneously

Scaling allows you to grow outreach while maintaining performance.

## Key Takeaway

Campaigns are not static. They are iterative systems.

The most successful users continuously:

- Learn from results
- Improve their approach
- Run better campaigns over time
