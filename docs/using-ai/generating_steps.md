# Generating Steps
To generate steps using AI, follow these steps:
1. On the campaign page go to the Steps Tab

![Go to Steps Tab](/images/ai_generate_steps/gen_steps_1.png)
2. Under a specific step, click generate

![Click Generate](/images/ai_generate_steps/gen_steps_2.png)

:::tip
You can specify the direction AI you want to take by mentioning the context in the entry box. This will help the AI generate a more relevant step instead of a generic one.
:::

3. Wait for the AI to generate the step
![Wait for AI to Generate](/images/ai_generate_steps/gen_steps_3.png)
