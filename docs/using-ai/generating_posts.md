# Generating Posts
To generate posts using AI, follow these steps:
1. On the campaign page go to the Steps Tab
![Go to Steps Tab](/images/ai_generate_posts/gen_posts_0.png)

2. Under a specific post, upload a photo, add some short text on what you want to generate then click Generate Button
![Go to Steps Tab](/images/ai_generate_posts/gen_posts_1.png)

:::tip
Do not forget to add mention the target topic. This will help the AI generate a more relevant post.
:::

3. Wait for the AI to generate the post
![Generate Post](/images/ai_generate_posts/gen_posts_3.png)

4. Click the Preview button to see how the post will look in Linked In
![Generate Post](/images/ai_generate_posts/gen_posts_4.png)
